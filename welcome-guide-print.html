Tips to get the most out of it

Small habits that make SubTracker actually useful

🗓️

Do a full audit first

Add every subscription you can think of — streaming, software, cloud storage, news, fitness apps. Don't filter yet, just list everything. The total will surprise you.

📊

Use the category chart

After adding everything, check the pie chart. Most people find one category is way larger than expected. That's where to cut first.

📅

Watch for yearly traps

Yearly subscriptions feel cheap month-to-month but add up fast. SubTracker converts them all to monthly so you see the real impact.

📤

Export monthly

Hit "Export CSV" once a month and open it in a spreadsheet. Track how your total changes over time — cancellations show up fast.

🌐

Use EN/ES toggle

If you work in both English and Spanish, the language switch is in the top right. It's a full UI switch — every label, not just the interface chrome.

💱

Switch currencies anytime

Living in Colombia or Mexico? Switch to COP or MXN to see your costs in local currency. The rate updates when you toggle.

🔖

Bookmark the app URL — don't lose it

Your data is saved in your browser's localStorage at app.html. If you clear browser data, your subscriptions will be gone. Export to CSV regularly as a backup.
